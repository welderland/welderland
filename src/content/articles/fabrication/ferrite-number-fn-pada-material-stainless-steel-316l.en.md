Ferrite Number (FN) is an international standard measure (typically following the AWS A4.2 standard) used to quantify the ferrite content (sometimes referred to as delta-ferrite) within the microstructure of austenitic stainless steels, such as SS 316L.

It is important to distinguish between the following:

Austenite (γ): The primary phase of SS 316L; it is non-magnetic, ductile, and possesses excellent corrosion resistance.

Ferrite (δ): A secondary phase that is magnetic and harder—though more brittle—than austenite.

FN is not an exact percentage of ferrite volume, but rather a value that correlates very closely with it. In many practical contexts, FN is considered equivalent to the ferrite volume percentage.
