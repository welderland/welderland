  For a welder or welding inspector, the ability to read and interpret a Welding
  Procedure Specification (WPS) is a non-negotiable skill. Think of a WPS as the
  "recipe" for producing a strong, safe, and code-compliant weld. Just as a chef
  must follow a recipe to create a perfect dish, a welder must follow a WPS to
  consistently produce quality welds.


  Without a solid understanding of WPS, the risks of weld failure, rework, and even workplace accidents increase significantly. This guide will walk you through understanding WPS—from its definition to how to read and interpret it like a true professional.
